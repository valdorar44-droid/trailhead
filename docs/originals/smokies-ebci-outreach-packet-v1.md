## Letter 1 — EBCI Division of Commerce (Destination Marketing)

- To: `info@ebci.gov` — Attn: Division of Commerce, Destination Marketing (Sean confirms the exact recipient before sending)
- Subject: Compensated participation request — Great Smoky Mountains audio driving tour (Trailhead)

> Hello,
>
> My name is Sean [surname], and I am the developer of Trailhead, an overlanding and camping trip-planning app for iOS and Android. We are building a premium GPS-triggered driving tour of Great Smoky Mountains National Park, working title "Great Smoky Mountains: Ridges, Rivers & Living Memory." The tour covers four route chapters: the Mountain Crossing between Gatlinburg/Sugarlands and Oconaluftee/Cherokee over Newfound Gap Road; Little River and Cades Cove; the Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail; and the Foothills Parkway.
>
> Several chapters travel through the Cherokee homeland. The planned content touches Kuwohi, Cherokee place names, and the historic and ongoing relationship between the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians and the places along these roads.
>
> We have deliberately paused those sections before writing a single word of them. I am writing to ask whether the Division of Commerce — or a colleague in Destination Marketing — can point us to the right path for EBCI review and compensated participation. Specifically, we would like:
>
> 1. Review and guidance on story framing for the Cherokee-facing chapters.
> 2. Compensated participation by EBCI reviewers, writers, or storytellers for those chapters, including pronunciations of Cherokee place names.
> 3. A referral to the appropriate cultural review authority — including the EBCI Cultural Institutional Review Board — if formal review applies.
>
> Our commitments:
>
> - No Cherokee scripts, pronunciation guides, or narration have been drafted, and none will be drafted before approval.
> - We will not request or publish sacred, private, ceremonial, or restricted knowledge, and we will not source Cherokee cultural content from generic web summaries.
> - Participation will be compensated. Rather than naming a figure, we invite you to propose the terms for participation, review, and any ongoing involvement.
> - Ownership, reuse, attribution, and withdrawal terms will be agreed in writing before work begins. Approval is never inferred from silence.
>
> We can provide a route map, our source list, outlines of the held story entries, the intended audience, commercial terms, a data-handling plan, and our review workflow. Could you advise the correct first step and the right person or office to work with?
>
> Thank you for your time and guidance.
>
> Sean [surname]
> Trailhead — [email] — [phone]

> Hello,
>
> Trailhead is developing a commercial GPS-triggered driving tour of Great Smoky Mountains National Park ("Great Smoky Mountains: Ridges, Rivers & Living Memory") for iOS and Android. Parts of the proposed tour discuss Kuwohi, the Cherokee homeland, and Cherokee relationships to places along the Newfound Gap and Cades Cove routes.
>
> We have deliberately paused those sections before drafting any scripts or pronunciation guidance, and we are writing to ask whether this project falls within the Cultural Institutional Review Board's review of work involving EBCI people, lands, language, or cultural materials.
>
> If review applies, we would appreciate guidance on:
>
> 1. Whether this commercial audio tour falls within Cultural IRB review.
> 2. Whether an EBCI department or community organization should sponsor or co-own the cultural portions.
> 3. What proposal, consent, attribution, data-handling, and final-review materials you require.
> 4. Whether Cherokee place names or pronunciations require separate language-program review.
> 5. Whether we may retain reviewed scripts and recorded narration in an encrypted commercial production archive.
> 6. What ongoing review is expected when routes, scripts, narrators, or product versions change.
>
> We plan to compensate EBCI participation — reviewers, writers, or storytellers — and we invite the Board or the participants to propose the terms. We will not request or publish sacred, private, ceremonial, or restricted knowledge. We can provide the route map, source list, outlines of the held story entries, intended audience, commercial terms, a data-handling plan, and our review workflow.
>
> Could you advise the correct first step and required materials for a scope determination?
>
> Thank you,
>
> Sean [surname]
