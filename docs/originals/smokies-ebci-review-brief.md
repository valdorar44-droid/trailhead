# Great Smoky Mountains Original — EBCI Review Brief

Status: **draft for Trailhead review; not sent**

## Why Trailhead is asking

Trailhead is preparing a paid, GPS-triggered driving tour through Great Smoky Mountains National Park. The product includes a Mountain Crossing chapter between Sugarlands and Oconaluftee/Cherokee and a Little River/Cades Cove chapter.

The proposed work touches Cherokee homeland, Kuwohi, language and pronunciation, historic relationships to places in the park, and contemporary EBCI community context. The EBCI Cultural Institutional Review Board states that work involving EBCI people, lands, language, or cultural materials may require its review. Trailhead should request a scope determination before writing those portions.

## Current safeguards

- No Cherokee-focused narration script has been drafted.
- No pronunciation guide has been created.
- No culturally sensitive text has been sent to a narrator or TTS service.
- No competitor narration has been purchased, extracted, or copied.
- Three placeholder entries are blocked in code until review is approved.
- Trailhead will not request sacred, private, ceremonial, or otherwise restricted knowledge.
- A participating EBCI reviewer, writer, or storyteller will be compensated.

## Scope determination requested

Trailhead should ask the Cultural IRB:

1. Whether the proposed commercial audio tour falls within Cultural IRB review.
2. Whether an EBCI department or community organization should sponsor or co-own the cultural portion.
3. What proposal, consent, attribution, data-handling, and final-review materials are required.
4. Whether a separate language-program review is needed for Cherokee names or pronunciation.
5. Whether Trailhead may retain reviewed scripts and recorded narration in an encrypted commercial production archive.
6. What ongoing review is expected when a route, script, narrator, or product version changes.

## Participation options to offer

- Paid scope consultation before drafting.
- Paid cultural reviewer for claim selection, wording, and pronunciation.
- Paid EBCI writer or storyteller for selected entries.
- Recorded EBCI narration only if the participant and reviewing authority prefer it and sign a clear commercial-use agreement.
- EBCI-approved attribution in the product.
- Final prepublication review of the affected entries.
- A defined correction, withdrawal, and version-update path.

Compensation, ownership, reuse, attribution, and withdrawal terms must be written before work begins. Approval should never be inferred from silence.

## Draft outreach message

Subject: Scope determination for a compensated Great Smoky Mountains audio-tour project

Hello,

Trailhead is developing a GPS-triggered driving tour for Great Smoky Mountains National Park. A small part of the proposed tour would discuss Kuwohi, the Cherokee homeland, and Cherokee relationships to places along the Mountain Crossing and Cades Cove routes.

We have intentionally paused those sections before writing scripts or pronunciation guidance. We would like to ask whether the project requires review by the EBCI Cultural Institutional Review Board and which EBCI office or community partner should guide the work.

We plan to compensate EBCI participation and can support a paid reviewer, writer, or storyteller, depending on the process the EBCI recommends. We will not request or publish sacred, private, ceremonial, or restricted knowledge. We can provide the route map, source list, three held story outlines, intended audience, commercial terms, data-handling plan, and review workflow.

Could you advise on the correct first step and required materials for a scope determination?

Thank you.
